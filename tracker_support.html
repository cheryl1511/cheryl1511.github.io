Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does Followers & Unfollowers Tracker work?

Our app provides real-time insights into your follower relationships (e.g., who stopped following you, who is not following you back, and new followers). Unlike cloud services, calculations are computed locally on your physical device using standard public API responses without storing your personal data on external servers.

2. Is my account and password safe?

Yes, absolutely. Followers & Unfollowers Tracker is engineered with strict on-device privacy. We do not operate remote databases and do not collect, transmit, or monetize your login credentials or personal information. Your session tokens remain securely stored inside your device's isolated sandbox storage.

3. Why aren't my follower numbers or analytics refreshing?

If your stats are not updating or appear unchanged:

  • Perform a Pull to Refresh on the main dashboard to fetch the latest data.
  • Ensure your device has an active, stable Internet connection.
  • If your session has expired, log out and log back in through the application to re-authenticate with the platform.
  • Third-party platform rate limits may temporarily delay updates if refreshed too frequently. Please allow a brief interval before refreshing again.

4. How do I restore my Pro / Premium subscription?

If you reinstalled the app or switched to a new device, you can easily restore your previously purchased subscription:

  • Open Followers & Unfollowers Tracker.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Tap Restore Purchases.
  • Make sure you are logged into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used to make the original purchase.

5. How do I manage, cancel my subscription, or request a refund?

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly and securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store). Developers cannot manually cancel subscriptions or process refunds directly on your behalf.

To cancel or manage on iOS (App Store):

  1. Open device Settings > tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select Followers & Unfollowers Tracker and choose Cancel Subscription.

To request a refund from Apple, visit: reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. How do I completely delete my data from the app?

Because all data is stored strictly on your local hardware:

  • Tap Log out in the app's Settings to instantly wipe all session cookies, profile caches, and local databases.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device permanently and irreversibly erases all local data associated with the app.
