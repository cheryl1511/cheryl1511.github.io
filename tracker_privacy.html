PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vigo Global ("we", "us", or "our") manages information in connection with the mobile application Followers & Unfollowers Tracker (the "App" or "Service"). We are fundamentally committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of your digital privacy.

By installing, launching, or using Followers & Unfollowers Tracker, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this document. If you have concerns about our approach to privacy, please refrain from using the application.

Our Core Principle: Zero Data Collection

Complete Local Privacy: Followers & Unfollowers Tracker is engineered as an on-device utility. We do not gather, transmit, store, monetize, or disclose your personal information or account credentials.

Unlike conventional web services, our architecture relies on local execution rather than cloud processing:

  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate proprietary servers, central databases, or remote endpoints designed to receive or archive user profiles, activity records, or account credentials.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not deploy third-party analytics trackers, surveillance tools, or advertising identifiers that log your individual usage behavior.
  • On-Device Computation: All follower comparisons, relationship calculations, and analytical insights are computed exclusively within the application environment on your physical smartphone or tablet.

Handling of Sensitive Information and Session Data

To enable account analytics, authentication tokens and web session identifiers (cookies) may be retained strictly within your device's isolated application sandbox. This technical data is utilized exclusively for direct communication between your personal device and the platform's public APIs.

At no stage is this credential data relayed to Vigo Global or any unauthorized external entity. You retain unconditional control over your local data: performing an in-app Logout immediately flushes all cached cookies and databases, and uninstalling the App irreversibly removes all related local storage files from your device.

Data Security

Because your sensitive details remain on your personal hardware and are never uploaded to remote servers, server-side data leaks or unauthorized data access from our infrastructure are virtually non-existent. We encourage you to utilize device-level security features (such as secure lock screens and biometrics) to safeguard your local device environment.

Children's Privacy Protection

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount importance. Followers & Unfollowers Tracker is designed for general audiences and does not intentionally target, solicit, or collect personal information from minors under the age of 13 (or under 16 where local jurisdictions apply), in full accordance with COPPA and global child protection standards.

Because the application does not collect personally identifiable data from any user, no personal information belonging to minors is ever accumulated or retained.
